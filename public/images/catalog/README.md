# Individual collection and product images

Created with the built-in image-generation tool to replace the old stretched
contact-sheet crops. The page uses individual responsive Next.js images with
preserved aspect ratios and garment-specific crop positions. The product dialog
shows the complete portrait using `contain`.

The six product images depict a black performance T-shirt, black training tank,
black performance shorts, black hoodie, taupe shell jacket and grey lounge pants.
The collection images distinguish performance, everyday essentials, minimal
staples, fitted activewear, outerwear and Exclusive eveningwear.

These are generated concept images matched to the named garment categories,
not photographs of verified production inventory. Real product specifications
and final client campaign approvals remain external to this frontend.

## Resolution

4K (2732 × 4096) was requested from the generator. The delivered originals are
1023 × 1537 pixels. They are **not native 4K**, and have not been upscaled.
The source dimensions were checked directly from all twelve PNG files.

Prompts and filenames are recorded in `prompts.json` and `bold-prompts.json`.
Inner Affair now uses `world-inner-underwear.png`: a dedicated underwear product
photograph of black cotton boxer briefs, replacing the base-layer/loungewear image.
Generated with the built-in image tool. Prompt summary: three black cotton boxer
briefs, one unfolded and two folded, on warm taupe stone; bold side lighting,
visible garment construction, empty upper space for the existing card title;
no models, logos or text. Exclusive retains dramatic burgundy satin tailoring.
